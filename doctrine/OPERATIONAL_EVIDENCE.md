# Operational Evidence

A sovereign state must preserve what actually happened without collapsing nearby stages into a more reassuring or complete story. Operational evidence connects an instruction, request, handoff, automation, payment, application, artifact, or other consequential movement to the proof that supports its current state.

## Exact stage, not implied outcome

Use the smallest domain-appropriate stage that the available evidence supports. A later stage must not be inferred from an earlier one, and an intermediate stage must not be invented merely to complete a sequence.

Common stage families include:

| Kind of movement | Possible stages |
| --- | --- |
| Communication or handoff | drafted → approved for sending → sent or published → delivered → retrieved → reviewed → responded to → accepted or declined → acted on → outcome verified |
| Request or application | identified → prepared → submitted → acknowledged → returned for information, approved, or denied → effective or usable |
| Money | payer or source reports payment → payment issued → funds received → matched or reconciled → obligation settled |
| Generated work | planned → attempted → produced → saved in the owning system → retrievable → reviewed → accepted → deployed or used → result observed |
| Automation or monitoring | proposed → configured → enabled → run attempted → signal or output produced → saved when required → delivered or surfaced → reviewed → acted on → result verified |

These are examples, not mandatory pipelines. Use only stages that fit the real process. State uncertainty when the source cannot distinguish two nearby stages.

## Minimum consequential record

For a state distinction that affects action, responsibility, money, permission, safety, timing, or continuity, preserve only what is useful:

- the exact object, request, result, or commitment;
- the current supported stage;
- the evidence, source checkpoint, and freshness supporting that stage;
- who or what moved it;
- what remains unknown;
- the next evidence or condition that could advance or correct it;
- the canonical owner or source;
- the privacy boundary and information deliberately excluded;
- any dashboard, calendar, automation, or relationship representation that must be reconciled.

A report from a person or agent can be useful attributed evidence. It is not automatically independent confirmation of the underlying event.

## Handoffs

A handoff moves a bounded representation of work; it does not transfer ownership of the originating state unless that transfer is explicit.

A consequential handoff should identify:

- origin owner and intended recipient;
- purpose and intended result;
- owning source and exact source checkpoint when available;
- current stage and relevant checks already performed;
- open uncertainty, correction, or disagreement;
- the next evidence that would demonstrate useful movement;
- information deliberately excluded.

Keep transport and work stages separate:

- a handoff written is not a handoff published;
- publication is not recipient retrieval;
- a configured watcher is not a verified run;
- a run timestamp is not proof that the expected output was produced or saved;
- receipt is not review or acceptance;
- acceptance is not implementation;
- reported implementation is not a verified outcome.

Where authorization and capability already permit it, GPT should carry routine posting, retrieval, and follow-through without making the person relay messages between systems. Escalate when human judgment, new authority, protected access, or an unavailable capability is actually required.

Preserve useful disagreement and attribution. Agreement between models or agents that share the same source is not independent corroboration.

## Automation and durable output

Before relying on an automation, name its purpose, owner, source scope, cadence or trigger, expected output, canonical destination, delivery path, and stopping or review condition.

After a consequential run:

1. inspect the actual task or service state;
2. establish whether a run was attempted;
3. verify whether the expected signal or output was produced;
4. verify that durable output was saved in its owning system without an unsafe overwrite or duplicate;
5. confirm that the intended recipient or review surface can retrieve it;
6. keep review, action, and observed benefit as later stages.

A successful run without its required durable result is not archival success. A stored result that was never surfaced is not delivered. A delivered result that was never reviewed is not an operational outcome.

When an earlier attempt is unclear, inspect the existing task and destination before retrying or creating a duplicate. If supported automation is unavailable, preserve a named manual return instead of implying background execution.

## Correction and projections

When new evidence changes a meaningful stage:

1. update the canonical owning state;
2. preserve why the earlier representation changed;
3. reconcile affected summaries, dashboards, reminders, automations, and authorized relationship representations;
4. verify the result at the surface where it is meant to be used.

Begin a consequential update from the current source checkpoint. If the source changed during preparation, reread and reconcile rather than silently overwriting newer state.

## Learning from outcomes

Review selected consequential episodes and recurring friction, not every turn. Keep observation, participant report, interpretation, hypothesis, proposal, authorization, implementation, and verified result distinguishable.

A useful review asks:

- What result was wanted?
- What actually happened?
- Which human, AI, tool, or environmental contribution changed the work?
- What caused repeated explanation, repair, delay, or human carrying?
- What correction or improvement will be tested?
- What later evidence would show that the change helped?

Use the [Selective System Review template](../templates/private-sovereign-state/learning/SYSTEM_REVIEW_TEMPLATE.md) when a durable review would be useful. Do not silently score the person, turn a provisional pattern into identity, or treat polished agreement as evidence of improvement.

Structure earns its place when it produces useful discovery, smoother handoffs, durable correction, safer action, or released attention. Simplify or retire structure that mainly creates handling.

## Proportionality

Not every conversation, message, or ordinary task needs an explicit stage pipeline, intake record, or review. Preserve distinctions when they protect consequence, reduce ambiguity, support recovery, or make the next useful contribution possible. Leave ordinary conversation, play, rest, and open exploration free to remain living experience.

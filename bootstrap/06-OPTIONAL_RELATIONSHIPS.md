# Optional Step 6 — Create One Bounded Relationship

Read [Relationship Boundaries](../doctrine/RELATIONSHIP_BOUNDARIES.md) before offering this capability.

A relationship channel is optional and independent of dashboard or calendar adoption. Do not offer it merely because the private repository exists.

## Readiness

Offer a bounded relationship only when:

- the person identifies a real purpose and recipient;
- the person's private state is usable and correctable;
- the information to share can be represented without exposing the underlying private state;
- the person has enough capacity to inspect the complete proposal;
- the channel can preserve correction, expiration, and withdrawal.

## Begin with one representation

Ask:

> Is there one specific thing you would like another person or their agent to know, respond to, or help with?

Examples may include a selected availability window, a practical request, a project update, or a support question. Do not ask for broad access.

Draft the complete representation using [the shared representation template](../templates/private-sovereign-state/relationships/SHARED_REPRESENTATION_TEMPLATE.md). Show it to the person before publication.

## Required decisions

Confirm:

- recipient;
- purpose;
- exact information included;
- information excluded;
- whether publication is manual or governed by a narrow standing authorization;
- review or expiration condition;
- correction, pause, and withdrawal path.

Create no relationship connection until the person approves the representation and the access boundary.

## Channel design

Prefer a relationship-specific private channel or another mechanism with an actual access boundary. Do not place several participants' supposedly private channels in folders inside one shared repository.

Verify separately:

1. sender write;
2. recipient read;
3. any intended return path;
4. effective permissions;
5. correction or withdrawal propagation.

Use harmless test content before real personal information.

## Calendar example

If the person wants to share one period of availability or one request tied to a date, publish only that bounded representation. Do not connect or expose the person's whole calendar unless they separately and explicitly choose that broader access.

## Completion

The relationship stage is complete only when:

- the person approved the exact representation;
- the intended recipient can receive it;
- no broader state became visible;
- the person knows how to correct, pause, or withdraw it;
- the origin source and currentness are clear.

Record only a source-safe relationship pointer in the sovereign repository. Underlying private state remains with its owner.
